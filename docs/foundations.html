Foundations

Sixteen grays and two typefaces.

Sixteen grays are the palette. There are three stroke weights, thirteen drawn icons and one kind of change: a press. To try other typefaces, open the themer.

Levels

Sixteen grays, and no more

The palette is sixteen grays, from 0x00 to 0xFF in steps of 0x11, like ink ground with more or less water. Every color in Ink is one of them, and tools/check-stillness.mjs fails anything else. Each mode uses six, so every page keeps one quiet register.

Semantic tokens

What each level is for

Components never name a level. They read these, so markup moves between modes untouched.

TokenPaperNightUse
--surface-canvas#eeeeee#111111The page.
--surface-sunk#dddddd#222222A recessed area, a code block.
--text-body#111111#eeeeeeEverything read. Headings too.
--text-secondary#444444#bbbbbbCaptions and hints. Still 7:1.
--mark-muted#777777#777777Idle marks: leader dots. Never type.
--rule#999999#555555A decorative separator.
--border-control#111111#eeeeeeThe edge of anything you touch.
--selected-fill#111111#eeeeeePressed and selected: the inversion.

Text clears 7:1 on every ground, which is AAA, so it reads for tired eyes, low vision and any light. Control edges clear 4.5:1 and idle marks 3:1. tools/check-contrast.mjs measures every pair in both modes, at both ranges.

Range

Softened, or full

By default Ink keeps Ahimsa’s law: the ground stops at #eeeeee and the ink at #111111, so there is no glare and no smear. For the strongest contrast, add ink-full to <html> and the grounds open to pure paper and pure ink. Displays that report themselves as grayscale get the full range by themselves.

Type

The book and the device

Lora · reading · --font-book

Simplicity, simplicity

Lora is a contemporary serif with brushed curves and moderate contrast, steady at reading sizes and warm without fuss. Ink sets it at weight 450 rather than 400, because sixteen-level anti-aliasing thins every stroke a little, and headings at 650.

Paragraphs are indented, not spaced, as in a book. Text is ragged right, hyphenated where the language allows, and runs the full width it is given unless --measure caps it.

Strokes

Depth is a rule and a margin

There are no shadows. A surface is separated by a rule, a border or space. Three strokes, never thinner than one pixel.

--stroke 1px · rules and cards
--stroke-control 2px · anything you touch
--stroke-focus 3px · the focus ring

Icons

Drawn on the pixel grid

Twenty-four units, a two-unit stroke on whole coordinates, round caps and joins, and no fills except the dots in contents. At 24px every line lands on a pixel, so it stays crisp. Each icon is symmetric unless the object is not, and each takes its ink from the text around it.

Change

There is no motion

Press either button. It inverts at once and comes back when you let go. That is the whole vocabulary of change. It has no duration, so there is nothing to watch, only something that happened. There are no motion tokens, and nothing to reduce for reduced motion.
