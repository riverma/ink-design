Actions

Buttons and chips

Button

One primary per view: the thing the page is for. Every button is at least 48px square, so it is easy to hit with a finger or a shaking hand. Disabled goes dashed, not faint, so it stays readable.

Reading

Pages, not scroll

.ink-page is a running head, the text and a folio. .ink-pager turns it, with the count always in view, and the controls never move between pages. When the set runs out, .ink-end says so. Try it.

WaldenWhere I Lived, and What I Lived For

I went to the woods because I wished to live deliberately, to front only the essential facts of life, and see if I could not learn what it had to teach, and not, when I came to die, discover that I had not lived.

Our life is frittered away by detail. An honest man has hardly need to count more than his ten fingers, or in extreme cases he may add his ten toes, and lump the rest. Simplicity, simplicity, simplicity!

Time is but the stream I go a-fishing in. I drink at it; but while I drink I see the sandy bottom and detect how shallow it is. Its thin current slides away, but eternity remains.

I never found the companion that was so companionable as solitude.

If a man does not keep pace with his companions, perhaps it is because he hears a different drummer. Let him step to the music which he hears, however measured or far away.

End of the excerpt

Five pages from Walden, by Henry David Thoreau, 1854.

Fields

Feedback

Notices that stay put

Bookmarked. Page 113, Solitude.
Your notes didn’t sync. Nothing is lost. They will sync the next time you connect.

A notice sits in the page where the person caused it. It never slides in, never times out and is never announced assertively. A toast decides when the reader has read it; a notice lets them decide.
